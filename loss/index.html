Loss of Sonnet 18

This is the installation as it ran in the SIGGRAPH 2024 Art Gallery. Screen 1 plays the last two lines of Shakespeare’s Sonnet 18 through 999 rounds of compression and blur, in six sequences. Screen 2 shows what a machine still reads at each step: the recognised words and their boxes.

Both screens follow the clock, so wherever and whenever they are opened they show the same moment. Here they sit side by side; in the gallery they were two monitors.
